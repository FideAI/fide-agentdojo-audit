The candidate exports no upstream implementation tree or raw corpus. The explicit allowlist includes derived numerical/Boolean evidence, relative public source locators and hashes, selected Fide analysis scripts, and support code. Fixture scripts contain references to upstream tasks and authored harmless fixture text. Dependencies are acquired separately under their own terms. A complete dependency-license inventory and all third-party content rights have not been cleared.

Default projections remove historical messages, injection text, sender/recipient addresses, tool-call arguments, receipts, free-text errors and local absolute paths. Reproduction outputs can reintroduce fixture addresses and historical content through raw analysis; those outputs are private and omitted from export. Hashes and relative public paths remain locators, not anonymization guarantees. No claim is made that an automated pattern scan proves absence of all sensitive content.

Before any public distribution, an accountable human must decide the Fide license, review source/data attribution and redistribution terms, review the exact allowlist and sensitive-data risks, adjudicate scientific qualifications, and separately authorize release. Public availability of a source and successful automated validation do not lift these gates.
